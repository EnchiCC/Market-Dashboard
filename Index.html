Four things to check before quoting any of this

Each was found in the exports themselves. Two of them change what the data can support.

One file duplicated Germany

Files 12 and 14 held byte-for-byte identical data apart from floating-point noise. File 14 was dropped. If it was meant to be a ninth market, that market never arrived.

France and Italy year-on-year is unusable

Both fall 55% to 70% on every reporting period while Germany, Spain and the Netherlands hold flat or grow. That pattern is a source-side category scope change, not a market event. Current levels are kept; the comparison is suppressed and shown as a flag in the strip above.

France and Spain could be the wrong way round

The exports carry no country field. Six markets are pinned by locale, size and country-exclusive brands. France and Spain sit within $0.8m of each other and rest on brand fingerprints alone. Nothing else in the analysis moves if they swap.

Each export lists a top twenty only

Coverage runs from 58% of the market in Spain to 89% in Canada. The remainder is a real long tail of channel-native brands — and it is where ASUS currently sits.

Source: Amazon Market Share Grid of Retail Sales, eight country exports, week 30 of 2026. Local currency converted at period-average rates: 1.165 US dollars per euro, 1.345 per pound, 0.715 per Canadian dollar.

Market totals are the median across all listed brands of sales divided by share, which is robust to the two-decimal rounding in the source. Global market size assumed at $4.5bn, the median of six published 2025 and 2026 estimates ranging from $2.2bn to $11.6bn. Addressable region assumed at 61% of global. Unit figures assume $95 per unit globally and $78 in this channel; the exports carry revenue only.
